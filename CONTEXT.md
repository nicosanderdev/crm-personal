# Personal CRM

A private circle of people you want to keep in touch with, and a record of the conversations between you.

## Language

**Person**:
Someone in your network — a friend, relative, colleague, or anyone else you intend to stay in contact with.
_Avoid_: Contact, lead, customer, account

**Organization**:
A string on a Person naming a workplace, school, club, or similar. Not its own record.
_Avoid_: Company, account

**Interaction**:
A past conversation with a Person: when it happened, through which channel, and notes on where it was going.
_Avoid_: Event, activity, touch, meeting, note

**Channel**:
How an Interaction happened — in person, call, video, message, email, or other.
_Avoid_: Medium, method

**Tier**:
How often you intend to reach a Person (cadence), not how important they are.
_Avoid_: Priority, status, rank, closeness

**Queue**:
The People who are due a conversation because their last Interaction is older than their Tier interval, or who have never been contacted.
_Avoid_: Inbox, tasks, reminders, pipeline

**Snooze**:
A temporary delay that hides a Person from the Queue until a chosen date, without logging an Interaction.
_Avoid_: Skip, postpone, reschedule

**Pause**:
Hiding a Person from the Queue indefinitely while keeping their record and history.
_Avoid_: Archive, mute, delete, unsubscribe

**Talking point**:
The current thread to pick up next time you reach a Person. It lives on the Person, not on a past Interaction.
_Avoid_: Follow-up, next action, agenda

**Dates**:
The upcoming timeline of Occasions and birthdays — a reason to reach someone because a day is coming, not because their cadence is due.
_Avoid_: Calendar, agenda, events

**Occasion**:
A one-off date you created, optionally tagged so you can find People who share those tags.
_Avoid_: Event, appointment, calendar entry

**Birthday**:
A Person's yearly date, shown on Dates for the next 90 days. Not an Occasion.

**Tag**:
A lowercase label on a Person or an Occasion. On an Occasion it joins to People who have the same tag.
